# Who were the first people in space?

And which spaceships did they fly in?

*A journey through time – from 1957 until today*

<!-- notes: Audience: 4th grade. Start by asking "Who do you think was first?" Use arrow keys or the clicker to move on; "i" shows the picture source. -->

---

# The first satellite: Sputnik 1

![Replica of the Sputnik 1 satellite in a museum](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/be/Sputnik_asm.jpg)
<!-- source: Sputnik 1 – NASA/NSSDC (Wikimedia Commons) | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sputnik_asm.jpg | Public domain (NASA) -->
<!-- year: 1957 -->

No people on board yet: this metal ball was the **first satellite** to circle the Earth, beeping as it went.
Things moved fast after that.

<!-- notes: Mention: a few weeks later the dog Laika flew on Sputnik 2 – the first animal in orbit. -->

---

# Yuri Gagarin – the first human in space

![Soviet stamps from 1961 showing Yuri Gagarin and a rocket](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/2c/The_Soviet_Union_1961_CPA_2560-2562_stamps_%28World%27s_First_Manned_Space_Flight%29.jpg)
<!-- source: Soviet Union stamps for Gagarin's flight, 1961 (Wikimedia Commons) | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Soviet_Union_1961_CPA_2560-2562_stamps_(World%27s_First_Manned_Space_Flight).jpg | Public domain -->
<!-- year: 12 April 1961 -->

Spaceship: **Vostok 1** – a ball just over 2 metres wide.
The 27-year-old Russian flew **once around the whole Earth** – in just 108 minutes! Shortly before landing he jumped out and came down by parachute.

---

# Alan Shepard – the first American in space

![Launch of the Mercury-Redstone 3 rocket with the Freedom 7 capsule](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/04/Launch_of_Freedom_7_-_GPN-2000-000859.jpg/1280px-Launch_of_Freedom_7_-_GPN-2000-000859.jpg)
<!-- source: Launch of "Freedom 7" – NASA (Wikimedia Commons) | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Launch_of_Freedom_7_-_GPN-2000-000859.jpg | Public domain (NASA) -->
<!-- year: 5 May 1961 -->

Spaceship: **Mercury "Freedom 7"**.
The flight lasted only 15 minutes: up into space and straight back down, without circling the Earth.

---

# John Glenn – the first American in orbit

![John Glenn climbing into the Friendship 7 capsule](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/10/John_Glenn_Entering_Friendship_7_-_GPN-2000-000652.jpg/1280px-John_Glenn_Entering_Friendship_7_-_GPN-2000-000652.jpg)
<!-- source: John Glenn entering "Friendship 7" – NASA (Wikimedia Commons) | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:John_Glenn_Entering_Friendship_7_-_GPN-2000-000652.jpg | Public domain (NASA) -->
<!-- year: 20 February 1962 -->

Spaceship: **Mercury "Friendship 7"**.
Glenn circled the Earth **three times** – in just under five hours.

---

# Valentina Tereshkova – the first woman in space

![Soviet stamp from 1963 showing Valentina Tereshkova and the Vostok 6 spaceship](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e6/The_Soviet_Union_1963_CPA_2886_stamp_%28Second_%27Team%27_Manned_Space_Flight._Valentina_Tereshkova_and_the_%27Vostok_6%27_spacecraft%29_large_resolution.jpg)
<!-- source: Soviet Union stamp, 1963 (Wikimedia Commons) | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Soviet_Union_1963_CPA_2886_stamp_(Second_%27Team%27_Manned_Space_Flight._Valentina_Tereshkova_and_the_%27Vostok_6%27_spacecraft)_large_resolution.jpg | Public domain -->
<!-- year: 16 June 1963 -->

Spaceship: **Vostok 6**.
She was 26 years old and stayed in space for **almost three days** – 48 laps around the Earth!

---

# Alexei Leonov – the first spacewalk

![Soviet stamp from 1965: the first spacewalk with Voskhod 2](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/5a/Soviet_Union-1965-Stamp-0.10._Voskhod-2._First_Spacewalk.jpg/1280px-Soviet_Union-1965-Stamp-0.10._Voskhod-2._First_Spacewalk.jpg)
<!-- source: Soviet Union stamp, 1965 (Wikimedia Commons) | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Soviet_Union-1965-Stamp-0.10._Voskhod-2._First_Spacewalk.jpg | Public domain -->
<!-- year: 18 March 1965 -->

Spaceship: **Voskhod 2**.
Leonov floated **freely in space** for about 12 minutes. His suit puffed up like a balloon – he had to let air out so he could fit back inside!

---

# Apollo 11 – the first people on the Moon

![Astronaut Buzz Aldrin on the Moon, with Neil Armstrong reflected in his visor](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/98/Aldrin_Apollo_11_original.jpg/1280px-Aldrin_Apollo_11_original.jpg)
<!-- source: Buzz Aldrin on the Moon (photographed by Neil Armstrong), Apollo 11 – NASA (Wikimedia Commons) | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Aldrin_Apollo_11_original.jpg | Public domain (NASA) -->
<!-- year: July 1969 -->

The giant **Saturn V rocket** carried Neil Armstrong, Buzz Aldrin and Michael Collins to the Moon.
Armstrong and Aldrin landed in the lunar module "Eagle". Look in Buzz's visor: that's Neil taking the photo!

---

# Sigmund Jähn – the first German in space

![Soviet stamp from 1978: Soyuz 31 and Salyut 6 in space](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/88/The_Soviet_Union_1978_CPA_4882_stamp_%28Soviet-East_German_Space_Flight._Soyuz_29_undocking_from_Salyut_6-Soyuz_31_complex%2C_photographed_from_Salyut_6%29.jpg)
<!-- source: Soviet Union stamp, 1978: Soyuz 31 at Salyut 6 (Wikimedia Commons) | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Soviet_Union_1978_CPA_4882_stamp_(Soviet-East_German_Space_Flight._Soyuz_29_undocking_from_Salyut_6-Soyuz_31_complex,_photographed_from_Salyut_6).jpg | Public domain -->
<!-- year: 26 August 1978 -->

Spaceship: **Soyuz 31**.
Together with Valery Bykovsky he flew to the space station "Salyut 6" and stayed in space for about a week.

---

# Yang Liwei – the first Chinese in space

<!-- year: 15 October 2003 -->

Spaceship: **Shenzhou 5** ("Divine Vessel").
After Russia and the USA, China became the third country to send people into space with its own rocket.

# And today? The space station ISS

![The Crew Dragon spaceship approaching the International Space Station](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/d0/The_SpaceX_Crew_Dragon_Endeavour_approaches_the_International_Space_Station_%28iss065e002661%29.jpg/1280px-The_SpaceX_Crew_Dragon_Endeavour_approaches_the_International_Space_Station_%28iss065e002661%29.jpg)
<!-- source: Crew Dragon "Endeavour" approaching the ISS – NASA (Wikimedia Commons) | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_SpaceX_Crew_Dragon_Endeavour_approaches_the_International_Space_Station_(iss065e002661).jpg | Public domain (NASA) -->
<!-- year: today -->

Since the year 2000, **people have always been living** on the ISS, about 400 km above us. It circles the Earth every 90 minutes.
In the photo, the "Crew Dragon" spaceship is flying up to the ISS.

Any questions?
